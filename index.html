FAQ

Common questions

What is Adiox?

Adiox is a gamified prop-trading and trading-competition portal. You register, fund one wallet and buy access to challenges, tournaments, trading games or the Training Room. Each one gives you a trading account on the Adiox Trading Platform, and the system tracks it live against the rules — you pass or fail, win prizes, or become a funded trader who receives a share of the profit.

Is the money on my trading account real?

No. Every product account is funded with virtual money on the Adiox Trading Platform. What is real is the money in your wallet: deposits, prizes, game winnings, funded-account payouts and referral commission.

What happens if I break a rule?

The account is eliminated: open trades are closed and the account is locked automatically. Entry fees are not refunded. Tournaments allow a paid rejoin where the organiser has enabled it.

How do I withdraw?

Complete identity verification (KYC), then request a withdrawal of at least $10 to crypto or a bank account. The amount is reserved from your balance straight away, you can edit or cancel while it is pending, and our team approves the payout.

Can I practise before buying a challenge?

Yes — the Training Room gives you a practice prop-firm account with preset or custom rules from $10. It is pass or fail only, and you can cancel within 5 minutes for a refund.

Where do I trade?

On the Adiox Trading Platform web terminal at trade.adiox.io. Use the Start Trading button on any account page in the portal; your login and password are shown there and emailed to you.
