QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

What is Adiox?

Adiox is a unified prop trading ecosystem built around four stages: Learn, Train, Compete, Trade. Traders study with an AI coach, practice prop-firm-style evaluations in a simulator, compete in tournaments and PvP trading games, and graduate to funded challenges, copy trading, signals, and professional risk tools — all inside one account.

Is Adiox free to start?

Yes. Creating an Adiox account is free and requires no card. The free tier includes Academy lessons, free tournaments, the trading glossary, and community access. Paid options — simulator runs from $10, evaluations, and Pro subscriptions — unlock as you progress, so you only pay when you're ready to level up.

What does Learn, Train, Compete, Trade mean?

They are the four stages of a trader's evolution on Adiox. Learn builds knowledge with the Academy and AI Coach. Train builds skill safely with PropSim, backtesting, and journaling. Compete proves skill in tournaments, PvP games, and leaderboards. Trade is the professional stage: funded challenges, copy trading, signals, and risk tools.

Can I get funded through Adiox?

Yes. Adiox offers performance-based evaluations — 1-step, 2-step, instant, and pay-later formats. Pass an evaluation on simulated capital and you qualify for a funded account with performance-based payouts. Trading involves risk and funding is never guaranteed; results depend entirely on your performance under the published rules.

Do I need experience to join Adiox?

No experience is required. Complete beginners start in the Adiox Academy with structured learning paths and an AI coach, then practice risk-free in the simulator before any money is at stake. Experienced traders can skip straight to evaluations, tournaments, PropSync multi-account management, or the copy-trading marketplace.

Which platforms does Adiox connect to (MT5, TradeLocker, Match-Trader)?

Adiox connects to MetaTrader 5 (MT5), TradeLocker, and Match-Trader. PropSync syncs and monitors accounts across multiple prop firms in one dashboard, while the Adiox Copier mirrors signals and copy-trading strategies to your connected platform accounts with your own risk settings applied.
