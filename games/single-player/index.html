Missions

Five missions, five habits

Each mission has its own entry fee, starting balance, leverage, duration and reward credit — shown before you start.

Stop Loss Master

Every trade needs a stop loss within 30 seconds of opening.

Daily Loss Defender

Never lose more than the set percentage in a single day.

Overall Loss Guardian

Never lose more than the set percentage in total.

Consistency Challenge

Finish in profit, with no single day making more than the set share of the total profit.

Precision Entry

Close at least the set number of trades with at least the set win rate.

Every mission

FAQ

Single player questions

What do I win?

Bonus credit, credited when you pass. The amount is shown on each mission before you pay. Credit covers part of future purchases and expires after 30 days.

Who checks the rules?

The Adiox Trading Platform enforces single player rules directly on your account, so a breach is applied the moment it happens.

Do I need to make a profit to pass?

Only where the mission says so — the Consistency Challenge requires finishing in profit. Every mission needs at least one closed trade.
