What the numbers on each listing mean

A good forex signal provider shows a real, recent track record. Here's how to read each listing before you subscribe.

Total R
The sum of closed results in units of risk. 1R is what a trade risks between entry and stop loss, so +12R means twelve times the risk per trade was gained over the window.
Win rate
The share of closed signals that ended in profit. Read it together with profit factor: a low win rate can still pay if winners are bigger than losers.
Profit factor
Gross profit divided by gross loss. Above 1.0 means the provider made more than it lost over the window.
Closed trades & window
How many signals closed, and over how many days. More trades over a longer window make the other numbers more meaningful.
Subscribers
How many traders follow the provider now. It shows popularity, not quality, so check the track record too.
Price & free trial
The fee per billing period, for example per 30 days. Some providers offer a free trial before you pay.

Quick checklist before you subscribe

  • Has a track record with enough closed trades, not just a few lucky ones.
  • Shows positive total R and a profit factor above 1 over the window.
  • Trades the markets you want: look for XAUUSD if you want gold signals.
  • Sent a recent signal, so the channel is active.
  • Has a price you're comfortable with, or a free trial to test first.
