FAQ

Wallet questions

Why do I need KYC to withdraw?

Identity verification protects your funds and is required before any withdrawal. You can deposit, play and trade without it. Start it from the KYC page in the portal; the result usually arrives automatically.

Can I withdraw bonus credit?

No. Bonus credit can only be used toward part of a purchase, and it expires after 30 days. Only the cash balance is withdrawable.

What happens to my balance when I request a withdrawal?

The amount is deducted when you submit the request, so it can't be spent twice. If you cancel while it's pending, or the request is rejected, it goes back to your balance.

Where can I see my history?

The wallet section of the portal has separate histories for deposits, withdrawals, deductions, credit received and credit used.
