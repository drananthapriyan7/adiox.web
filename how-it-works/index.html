How it works

One account, one wallet, live trading accounts.

Every Adiox product follows the same path: you pay once, receive a trading account on the Adiox Trading Platform funded with virtual money, and the system tracks it live against the product's rules.

Create account →Trading platform ↗

1 · Getting started

Set up in minutes

  1. Register

    Create your account and confirm your email address — confirmation is required before you can sign in.

  2. Log in

    Your login streak starts, and your first achievements are ready to track.

  3. Complete your profile

    Nickname, avatar, country and language. Completing it unlocks the $30 welcome credit.

  4. Verify your identity

    KYC is needed only when you want to withdraw.

2 · Buying any product

One checkout for everything

  1. Choose

    Open a challenge, tournament, game, mission or simulator and click Join, Start or Buy.

  2. Review the split

    The checkout shows how much comes from your coupon, credit, wallet and the payment gateway. Try it →

  3. Pay

    If a card, link or crypto payment is needed, your purchase completes once the payment is confirmed.

  4. Get your account

    Your trading account is created, and the login and password are shown and emailed to you.

3 · Trading

Trade on the Adiox Trading Platform

Accounts are opened on the Adiox Trading Platform. Press Start Trading on any account page to open the web terminal at trade.adiox.io — no download needed.

  • Live account pages refresh every few seconds and reload when the status changes.
  • Rules checked every 1–3 seconds. Break one and open trades are closed and the account is locked automatically.
  • Credentials kept safe — your trading password is stored encrypted, and you can reveal or reset it.
