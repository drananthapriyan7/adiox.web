Privacy Policy

Draft · pending legal review. This plain-language summary describes how Adiox works today; the binding legal text will replace it.

Data we collect

  • Account: email address, password (hashed), referral code and account status.
  • Profile: nickname, avatar, country and state, language and WhatsApp number, if you add them. Your country and dialling code may be suggested from your IP address.
  • Activity: logins, online status, notifications and an activity log including IP address.
  • Payments: deposit and withdrawal records. Card and crypto payments are processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Identity verification: the status and session of your KYC check, carried out by our verification provider.
  • Purchase checks: IP address and a device identifier, used to protect one-time offers such as the First-Buyer Offer.

Why we use it

To run your account and wallet, create and monitor trading accounts, process payments and withdrawals, verify identity, prevent fraud and abuse, send transactional emails and notifications, and answer support requests.

Sharing

We share data only with the providers needed to operate the service — the trading platform, payment providers, the identity-verification provider and our email service — and where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can update your profile at any time. To ask about access, correction or deletion of your data, contact support.
