1. Your account

You must be old enough to enter a binding contract where you live and provide accurate details. You need a confirmed email address to sign in. One person, one account; you are responsible for keeping your login and trading passwords safe.

2. What Adiox provides

Adiox offers prop-firm challenges, funded accounts, Instant Funded challenges, tournaments, Trading Duels and Battles, single player games and the Training Room simulator. Each gives you a trading account on the Adiox Trading Platform funded with virtual money. The rules of each product are published on its page before you pay, and the system applies them automatically.

3. Fees and refunds

  • Fees are shown before you pay and are charged through one checkout (coupon, bonus credit, wallet, then payment gateway).
  • Entry fees are not deposits and are not refunded once an account is created and the product has started, except: Training Room cancellation within 5 minutes; game lobbies that expire after 24 hours; a Duel draw (refunded minus commission); and automatic refunds when an account cannot be created after payment.
  • Breaking a product's rule ends the account without a refund.

4. Payouts, prizes and rewards

Funded-account payouts, tournament prizes, game winnings and rewards are paid only when the published rules are met, and payout requests are reviewed before approval. Bonus credit can only be used toward part of a purchase, is capped per purchase and expires after 30 days.

5. Wallet and withdrawals

Withdrawals require approved identity verification (KYC), have a $10 minimum and are paid to crypto or a bank account after review.

6. Fair play

Multiple accounts, collusion between players, abuse of offers (including the First-Buyer Offer) and any attempt to manipulate results are prohibited. Adiox may eliminate accounts, withhold prizes or close accounts that break these terms.

7. Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will publish the new version here. Questions: contact support.
