QUESTIONS

Tournaments, answered

How do trading tournaments work?

Every participant gets an identical simulated account for a fixed session — same starting balance, same markets, same rules. You trade the session, and a live leaderboard ranks everyone by risk-adjusted performance. When time expires, the top of the board splits the published prize pool.

Are trading competitions free to enter?

Daily tournaments on Adiox are free — no card, no entry fee, real prizes. Weekly championships and special events carry paid entry with proportionally larger published prize pools. Every event page shows the entry cost and prize split before you join.

How are tournament winners paid?

Prize splits are published before every event — typically 50% to first place, 30% to second, 20% to third, or a top-10 split in larger fields. Prizes are paid as cash, funded-challenge vouchers, or subscription credit, delivered to your Adiox account within 48 hours of results being verified.

Will I be matched against professionals?

No. Skill-based divisions — Bronze through Diamond — group you with traders at your own level, based on your tournament history and simulator record. You climb by winning your division, and prizes exist at every level, so beginners compete for real stakes from day one.
