STAGE 03 — PROVE SKILL

Trading competitions where skill is the only currency

What is a trading competition?

A trading competition is a skill-based event where participants trade identical simulated accounts under identical rules for a fixed period, ranked by performance. Prizes go to the top of the leaderboard. It recreates the pressure of live trading — with entry fees, not trading capital, as the only cost.

Practice without stakes eventually plateaus — you need pressure to find out what your discipline is really made of. The Compete stage is that pressure, engineered safely. Weekly free tournaments let anyone test themselves against the field for nothing; bigger events put published prize pools on the line. In the Arena, 1v1 duels strip trading down to its purest form: same market, same capital, same clock, best trader wins. And everything you achieve is recorded on public, verifiable leaderboards — a rank that follows you across the ecosystem and becomes your reputation when you later publish strategies or signals. No follower counts, no screenshots, no claimed results. Just a number next to your name that you earned where everyone could watch.

QUESTIONS

Competing on Adiox, answered

Are Adiox tournaments free to enter?

There are free tournaments every week that require no card and no payment — real prizes included. Larger competitions may have paid entry with bigger published prize pools. Every tournament page states the entry cost and prize structure upfront.

What can I win in trading competitions?

Prize pools are published before each event and can include cash prizes, funded-challenge vouchers, and subscription credit. Payouts go to the top of the leaderboard under rules stated on the tournament page. Prizes and availability vary by region.

Is competitive trading gambling?

Adiox competitions are skill-based: every participant trades the same markets with the same simulated capital and rules, and rankings are decided by trading performance, not chance. Paid-entry competitions may still be restricted in some jurisdictions — see our Regional Restrictions page.

How do 1v1 Arena duels work?

Two traders receive identical simulated accounts and a fixed time window on the same market. Best risk-adjusted performance wins the duel and the rating points. Duels are matched by skill rating, so you climb by beating traders at your own level.
