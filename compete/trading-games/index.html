ANATOMY OF A DUEL

How a match actually works

1

Matched by rank

The queue pairs you with an opponent at your rating. New to the Arena? Five placement matches set your starting division — you'll never be thrown to a Legend on day one.

2

Identical market replay for both players

Both accounts trade the same anonymized historical replay — same candles, same spreads, same news events, revealed in real time to both sides simultaneously. Neither player has seen it before.

3

Highest risk-adjusted P&L wins

Raw profit isn't the score — profit relative to the risk you took is. An over-leveraged coin-flip loses to a controlled, disciplined session even when it makes more money. That's the point.

THE IDENTICAL-CONDITIONS GUARANTEE

Why the Arena is skill, not chance

In most trading, the market's randomness is part of your result. In an Arena duel it cancels out: both players face the exact same prices at the exact same moments, with the same capital and the same rules. The only variable left is the decisions each player makes — position size, timing, risk control, and when to do nothing. That is why duels are decided by decision quality, not luck, and why we publish the replay ID of every match so results can be independently checked.

Same replay, verified

Each duel's market replay is hashed and published with the result. Anyone can confirm both players saw identical data.

Anti-collusion

Queue randomization, replay anonymization, and pattern detection stop win-trading and mirrored accounts.

Risk-adjusted scoring

The scoring formula punishes reckless leverage, so "all-in and pray" is a losing strategy by design.

QUESTIONS

The Arena, answered

What is a trading duel?

A trading duel is a 1v1 match where two traders receive identical simulated accounts and trade the exact same replayed market conditions for a fixed time window. Because both players see the same prices, the higher risk-adjusted P&L can only come from better decisions — the market's direction is identical for both.

Is PvP trading skill-based or gambling?

Arena duels are skill-based. Both players trade the same market replay with the same capital and rules, so chance is removed from the comparison: whatever the market does, it does it to both players. Outcomes are decided by decision quality, risk control, and timing. Paid modes may be restricted in some jurisdictions.

How does Arena matchmaking work?

New players complete five placement matches to receive a starting rank. After that, an ELO-style rating matches you with opponents at your level — Bronze plays Bronze, Legend plays Legend. Ratings adjust after every duel based on the opponent's strength, and divisions reset softly each season.

What do I win in the Arena?

Ranked wins earn rating, season points, and division emblems. Season rewards include cash prizes for top ladder finishers, funded-challenge vouchers, subscription credit, and cosmetic profile rewards. Casual and free duels award rating only — you never have to pay to climb.
