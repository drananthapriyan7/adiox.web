How to

How to win a free prop challenge account

Free trading challenges on Adiox come in the form of free tournaments: $0 to enter, ranked by final equity, with prizes that can include a challenge account.

  1. Create a free account

    Register and confirm your email. Complete your profile for the $30 welcome credit.

  2. Find a free tournament

    Open the tournaments list and look for a $0 entry fee and a prize table that includes a challenge account.

  3. Qualify and rank

    Make at least the minimum number of trades and stay inside the loss limits. Ranking is by final equity.

  4. Receive your prize

    Winning ranks receive their prize after the tournament ends — including a free challenge account where offered.

FAQ

Free challenge questions

Where can I start a forex prop challenge with no upfront fee?

On Adiox, win one: free tournaments have a $0 entry fee, and their prize tables can award a free challenge account. If you'd rather start a challenge directly, the lowest-cost options are the $4.95 First-Buyer Offer and Pay Later, which needs only a small upfront fee.

Are there free prop accounts on Adiox?

Not for direct purchase — challenges have an entry fee. A free challenge account can be won as a tournament prize, and tournament accounts in free tournaments cost nothing.

Are there free trading challenges on Adiox?

Yes — free tournaments are free trading challenges: a $0 entry fee, the same starting balance for everyone, and prizes by rank in cash, bonus credit or a free prop challenge account.

Can I use the $30 welcome credit for a challenge?

Yes, for part of it. Bonus credit can cover up to 50% of a purchase — each challenge shows its own limit — and expires after 30 days.

Are there coupons for challenges?

Current coupons are listed on the Promotions page in the portal. Coupons give a percentage off the fee, and on Pay Later challenges they reduce the activation fee too.
