How to find the best forex signal provider — and the best gold signal provider

By the Adiox team · Updated 27 November 2026 · Based on Adiox's published product rules · Editorial standards · Not investment advice

The best trading signal provider for you is the one with a long, complete track record, a maximum drawdown you could live through, and a stop loss on every signal. Judge providers on their full history — losing trades included — not on their best month. For gold (XAUUSD), also check how they size stops for gold's larger moves.

Gold (XAUUSD)

How to choose the best gold signal provider (XAUUSD)

Gold moves further than most currency pairs in the same time, so a good XAUUSD signal provider plans for it.

  • Wider stops, smaller size. Stops are set for gold's range and the lot size is reduced to keep risk per trade fixed.
  • Session awareness. Signals note whether they target the London or New York session.
  • News handling. The provider says how it treats major data releases.
  • Spread in the results. Results reflect realistic spreads, not ideal fills.

Signal provider questions

How can I compare gold signal providers using their trading history and drawdown?

Put providers side by side on track-record length, maximum drawdown, stop-loss use on every signal, win rate read with average reward:risk, and consistency across months. Rule out any provider that hides losing trades or increases size after losses. On Adiox, start from the top signal providers listed in the portal's Trade Copier overview.

Who is the best trading signal provider?

The best trading signal provider — forex or gold — depends on the drawdown and trading style you can follow. A provider with modest, steady results and a small drawdown is often easier to follow than one with a spectacular month and a deep drawdown. Use the eight-point scorecard on this page.

How do I find the best forex signal provider?

Shortlist providers with at least several months of complete history, then compare maximum drawdown, stop-loss use on every signal, and win rate read together with average reward:risk.
