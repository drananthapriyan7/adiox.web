How to pass a prop firm challenge

By the Adiox team · Updated 27 November 2026 · Based on Adiox's published product rules · Editorial standards · Not investment advice

Most prop firm challenges are failed on a loss rule, not on a missed profit target. To pass, size every trade so a single loss can't breach the daily loss limit, know exactly how your firm measures each limit, reach the profit target over the required trading days, and rehearse the rules before you pay.

Step by step

Nine steps to passing a prop firm challenge

  1. Read every rule before you pay

    Profit target, minimum trading days, overall loss, daily loss and inactivity. On Adiox they are shown on each challenge before checkout.

  2. Know how each limit is measured

    On Adiox, daily loss is measured from the day's opening balance at midnight UTC and overall loss from the starting balance.

  3. Turn the limits into dollar levels

    On $10,000 with 5% daily and 10% overall, your floors are $9,500 for the day and $9,000 overall. Write them down.

  4. Risk a small fraction per trade

    If one trade can lose 1% at most, you need five losses in a row to hit a 5% daily limit — time to stop and reset.

  5. Spread the target over the days

    An 8% target over several trading days is a few calm gains, not one big bet. You must also trade the minimum number of days.

  6. Watch your meters live

    Adiox account pages show progress toward the target and each loss limit as you trade, and checks run every few seconds.

  7. Rehearse first

    Run the same rules in the Training Room from $10 until you pass consistently, then buy the real challenge.

  8. Don't go idle

    15 idle days in a row ends an Adiox challenge account; warnings arrive at 7, 10 and 14 days.

  9. Stop after a bad day

    Revenge trading after a loss is the most common way daily limits are broken. End the session and return tomorrow.

Why traders fail

The five most common reasons

  • One oversized trade breaches the daily loss limit.
  • Revenge trading after a loss turns a small drawdown into a breach.
  • Misreading the limit — measuring from the wrong balance.
  • Rushing the target in the first days with too much size.
  • Going idle and forgetting the inactivity rule.
New to challenges? Read daily loss limit vs max drawdown first — it explains the rule that ends most attempts.

FAQ

Passing a challenge: questions

What is the hardest rule in a prop firm challenge?

For most traders it is the daily loss limit, because a single oversized trade or a revenge-trading session can breach it in minutes. Size each trade so several losses in a row stay inside it.

How long does it take to pass a prop firm challenge?

At least the minimum number of trading days set by the phase, and as long as you need to reach the profit target without breaking a loss limit. On Adiox, passing Phase 1 creates Phase 2 for free.

Should I practise before buying a challenge?

Yes. Rehearsing the exact rules is the cheapest way to find your weak spot. The Adiox Training Room runs a challenge simulator from $10, and you can cancel within 5 minutes for a refund.

What happens if I break a rule?

On Adiox the account is eliminated: open trades are closed and the account is locked, with no refund. You can buy a new challenge and start again.
