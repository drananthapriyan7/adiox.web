How to do copy trading in MT4/MT5: setup guide

By the Adiox team · Updated 27 November 2026 · Based on Adiox's published product rules · Editorial standards · Not investment advice

Copy trading on MT4 or MT5 links a master account — the one whose trades are copied — to one or more follower accounts. You choose the source, connect your follower account, set how lot sizes are calculated, map any symbols your broker names differently, and set a loss limit before you start. Step by step

How to copy trades on MT4 or MT5

Whichever copier you use, how to do copy trading in MT4/MT5 comes down to the same six steps.

  1. Choose what to copy

    Another trader's master account, a signal provider, or your own master account copied to your other accounts.

  2. Connect your follower account

    Enter the broker server exactly as it appears in your MT4 or MT5 login window, plus the account login and password.

  3. Pick a lot-size mode

    Fixed lot, a multiplier of the master's lot, a balance ratio between the accounts, or a percentage of balance risked per trade.

  4. Map symbols

    Brokers name instruments differently — XAUUSD, XAUUSD.m or GOLD. Map them so every trade lands on the right symbol.

  5. Set loss limits

    Cap the maximum lot and set a drawdown or daily loss limit that stops copying if it's hit.

  6. Start — and know how to stop

    Switch copying on and confirm the first trade arrives. Make sure you know where the stop switch is.

MT4 vs MT5

What changes between MT4 and MT5

Account loginSame idea on both: broker server, login and password.
Position modelMT5 accounts can be hedging or netting; a netting account merges positions in the same symbol, which changes how copied trades appear.
Copier typeSome copiers run as an Expert Advisor inside the terminal, so the terminal (or a VPS) must stay online. Cloud copiers run on a server instead.
Copying between themCheck that the copier supports both platforms if your master and follower are on different ones.

FAQ

Copy trading questions

How do I set up copy trading on MT5 and control my lot size?

Connect your MT5 follower account with its broker server, login and password, choose the master to copy, then pick a lot-size mode: a fixed lot, a multiplier of the master's lot, a balance ratio, or a percentage of balance risked per trade. Add a maximum lot and a drawdown limit before you switch copying on.

How to do copy trading in MT4/MT5?

Choose a master account or signal provider, connect your MT4 or MT5 follower account with its broker server, login and password, pick a lot-size mode, map any differently named symbols, set a maximum lot and a loss limit, then switch copying on. With Adiox, you find top masters in the portal and copy on copier.adiox.io.

How do I copy trades on MT4?

The steps are the same as MT5: choose a source, connect the MT4 follower account, set the lot-size mode, map symbols and set loss limits. If your copier runs as an Expert Advisor, the MT4 terminal must stay running.

What is a forex trade copier?

Software that reads trades from a source — a master account or a signal provider — and places matching orders on follower accounts, sized by the settings you choose.
