ADIOX COPIER · MT4 & MT5

Trade once. Copy everywhere.

The Adiox Copier is one execution layer for three ways of copying trades: follow other traders, synchronise your own MT4 and MT5 accounts, or turn Telegram signals into orders automatically. Lot size, risk, symbols, stop loss and take profit are all set by you, from one dashboard.

What is a trade copier?

A trade copier reads trades from a source (another trader's account, your own master account, or a Telegram signal channel) and places matching orders on your connected trading accounts, sized and filtered by the risk settings you choose. No manual entry, no missed alerts.

QUESTIONS

Adiox Copier, answered

What is the difference between Social Copier and Private Copier?

Social Copier lets you follow and copy trades from other traders (masters) ranked on Adiox. Private Copier copies trades between trading accounts you own or manage: one master account, any number of followers.

Which brokers and platforms are supported?

Telegram Copier works with any broker that gives you an MT4 or MT5 account. Social and Private Copier currently support MT5. You pick your broker server from a searchable list, exactly as it appears in your MT4/MT5 login window. PropSync dashboards additionally monitor TradeLocker and Match-Trader accounts.

Do I need to install anything or run a VPS?

Not for Telegram Copier. Copying runs on Adiox servers, so there is nothing to install and nothing of your own to keep online. Social and Private Copier plans are either hosted by Adiox or run on your own MT5; each plan states which.

Does Adiox provide the trading signals?

No. The Adiox Copier is execution software. It copies what a master account or a Telegram channel you already follow sends out. Masters are independent traders and channels are third parties. Adiox does not produce signals for the copier or give investment advice.

How are lot sizes calculated?

You choose. Telegram Copier can use a fixed lot, a percentage of balance risked per trade, a lot per fixed amount of balance, or the lot published in the signal, with separate sizing available for gold. Social and Private Copier can use a fixed lot, a multiplier of the master trade, a balance ratio between the two accounts, or a percentage risk.

Will copied trades fill at exactly the same price?

Not always. A copied order is sent to your broker and fills at your broker's price at that moment, so spread, latency and market conditions can move it. A market entry is only valid for about three minutes, so a late signal is skipped rather than filled at a much worse price.

Can I use the copier on a prop-firm challenge account?

Yes, and the risk limits are built for it: a daily loss cap that resets the next day, an overall loss cap in percent or currency, a per-trade drawdown close, and a profit target that stops trading once reached. You enter the numbers to match your firm, because the copier does not know your provider's rules by itself. Always check whether your firm permits copy trading.

Is my broker password safe?

Broker passwords are encrypted at rest, used only to sign in and start copying, and never written to logs. A master account connects with its read-only investor password, so an account being copied can never be traded from Adiox.

Can I pause copying?

Yes. Every account has a Stop switch that takes effect immediately. Nothing is queued while an account is stopped, so when you start it again only trades that arrive after that point are copied.

How does the free trial work, and will I be charged automatically?

Telegram Copier plans include a 3-day free trial. Plans are paid at checkout by card or crypto, and nothing renews or charges by itself. We remind you before your period ends, and you renew only if you want to.
