QUESTIONS

Copy trading, answered

How does copy trading work?

You pick a strategy from the marketplace and set an allocation. From then on, every trade the provider takes is mirrored to your connected account (MT5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader) proportionally to your allocation — executed in the cloud, 24/7, with your own risk limits applied on top. You can pause or stop copying at any time.

Is copy trading safe?

Copy trading always carries market risk — a strategy that made money last year can lose money this year. Adiox reduces the avoidable risks: every track record is verified on-platform (no edited screenshots), drawdown is displayed as prominently as gains, and your equity-protection stop closes all copied positions if losses hit your chosen limit.

How do strategy providers earn on Adiox?

Providers earn performance fees — a percentage of the profits their followers make, high-water-marked so fees are only charged on new profit. Private group providers can add subscription pricing on top. Adiox handles billing, distribution, and the verified track record; providers keep the majority share.

Do I need a VPS for copy trading?

No. Adiox copy trading runs entirely in the cloud: trades are mirrored server-side between the provider's account and yours, around the clock. There is no VPS to rent, no terminal to leave running, and no machine that can crash mid-trade.
