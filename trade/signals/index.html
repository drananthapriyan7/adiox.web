QUESTIONS

Signals, answered

How do I know a signal provider is legit?

On Adiox, you don't have to trust — you can check. Every signal a provider publishes is timestamped by the platform at the moment it's issued and tracked against real market prices afterward. Win rates, average return, and drawdown are computed by Adiox from that record. Edited screenshots, backdated calls, and deleted losers are impossible by design.

How does a trade copier work?

A trade copier turns a signal into an actual order on your account automatically. With the Adiox Copier you connect your MT5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader account, choose providers, and set your risk per trade. When a signal fires, the Copier sizes and places the order within your limits — including stop loss and take profit — in under a second, without you touching a terminal.

Can I receive signals without auto-execution?

Yes. Every subscription includes manual delivery — in the Adiox app, by Telegram, or over the API — with entry, stop, and target clearly stated. The Copier is optional; many subscribers start manual and enable auto-execution once they trust a provider's record.

How do signal providers earn on Adiox?

Providers set tiered subscription prices (for example: signals-only, signals + analysis, priority tier). Adiox handles billing and distribution and keeps a platform share; providers keep the majority. Because records are verified on-platform, honest providers convert followers dramatically better than screenshot marketing ever could.
