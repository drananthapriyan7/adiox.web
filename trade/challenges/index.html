    RULES & TRADING CONDITIONS

    Every rule, in plain language — with the reason it exists

    Daily drawdown limit (3–5% depending on model)

    Your account equity may not drop more than the daily limit below the day's starting balance. Breach it and the evaluation ends. WHY THIS RULE EXISTSIt stops one bad day from becoming a blown account. Professional risk desks impose the same limit on their own traders.

    Maximum overall drawdown (6–10%)

    Equity may never fall more than the overall limit below your starting balance, at any point in the evaluation or funded phase. WHY THIS RULE EXISTSThis is the capital provider's actual risk ceiling — it defines the most that can ever be lost backing you.

    Consistency requirement

    No single trading day may account for more than 40% of your total profit at the point of passing. WHY THIS RULE EXISTSIt filters out the one lucky over-leveraged trade. A funded account rewards a repeatable process, not a lottery ticket.

    News trading windows

    Positions opened or closed within 2 minutes of a high-impact news release don't count toward the profit target (they're not a breach — they just don't count). WHY THIS RULE EXISTSNews spikes are execution roulette. The rule keeps the evaluation about skill, and the News Filter can enforce it for you automatically.

    Permitted styles & instruments

    All styles welcome: scalping, swing, news-adjacent, EAs, and overnight/weekend holding on swing accounts. FX majors and minors, metals, indices, and crypto CFDs. WHY THIS RULE EXISTSIt doesn't restrict — it clarifies. We publish what's allowed so nothing surprises you after you've paid.

    QUESTIONS

    Challenges, answered

    What is a 1-step challenge?

    A 1-step challenge is a prop firm evaluation with a single phase: hit one profit target (10% on Adiox) while respecting drawdown limits, and you qualify for a funded account. It's faster than a 2-step evaluation but carries slightly tighter drawdown rules and a slightly higher fee for the same account size.

    What is a pay-later prop firm challenge?

    A pay-later challenge lets you start an evaluation with zero upfront fee. On Adiox you qualify through your PropSim simulator record, trade a standard 2-step evaluation, and the fee (1.5× the normal price) is deducted from your first funded payouts. If you never reach a payout, you owe nothing.

    What happens if I fail a challenge?

    The evaluation ends and your fee is not refunded (pay-later evaluations simply end with nothing owed). Your dashboard shows exactly which rule ended the attempt, your AI Coach explains the pattern behind it, and you can restart anytime — most traders practice the failure scenario in PropSim for $10 before paying for a new attempt.

    How fast are payouts processed?

    Payout requests are verified and processed within 48 hours. Funded traders on 2-step accounts keep up to 90% of simulated profits, and your first payout on a 2-step account also refunds your original challenge fee. Payout history is published on the winners wall.
