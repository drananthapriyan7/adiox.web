QUESTIONS

Protection, answered

What is a prop firm news rule?

A news rule restricts trading around high-impact economic releases (NFP, CPI, FOMC, central bank rate decisions). Common versions: no opening or closing positions within 2–5 minutes of the event, no holding positions through it, or profits from news-window trades not counting. Rules differ by firm — violating them can void profits or terminate a funded account, which is why the News Filter ships with per-firm presets.

How do I avoid a drawdown breach?

Three habits prevent most breaches: size positions from your remaining daily-loss budget (not from your account balance), stop trading well before the limit rather than at it, and automate the stopping. The Risk Manager does all three — it calculates safe position sizes, warns as you approach your threshold, and can auto-close everything before the firm's line is hit.

What is a daily loss lock?

A daily loss lock is a hard stop on your own trading: once your losses for the day reach a level you set — typically below the firm's actual limit — the tool closes open positions and blocks new orders until the daily reset. It converts the firm's account-ending rule into a survivable, self-imposed one.

Can I trade news on a funded account?

It depends entirely on your firm. Some allow news trading freely, many restrict opening or closing positions in a 2–5 minute window around high-impact events, and some prohibit holding through them. Check your firm's rules — or load its preset in the News Filter, which blocks only what your specific firm prohibits and leaves everything else alone.
