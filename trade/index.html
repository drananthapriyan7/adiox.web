STAGE 04 — EARN PROFESSIONALLY

Trade funded. Run it like an operation.

What is funded (prop) trading?

Funded trading means trading a firm's simulated capital instead of your own savings. You pass a performance evaluation — hitting profit targets within drawdown rules — and then earn performance-based payouts from a funded account. Your risk is the evaluation fee; the firm's capital absorbs the trading risk.

The Trade stage is where preparation converts into income — and where the stakes justify professional tooling. Adiox offers evaluations in every format the industry has produced: 1-step and 2-step challenges, instant funding, and pay-later entry, each with rules published in full before you commit. Around the funded account itself sits an operations layer most traders have never had: PropSync runs every prop account you own from one command deck, the Risk Manager hard-caps exposure before a breach can happen, and the News Filter blocks trades in restricted windows automatically. For traders with a proven edge, the copy-trading marketplace and Signal Hub turn verified leaderboard reputation into a second income stream. This is prop trading treated as a profession, not a lottery ticket.

QUESTIONS

Trading professionally on Adiox, answered

How do I get funded through Adiox?

Choose a challenge format — 1-step, 2-step, instant, or pay-later — and pass its evaluation rules on simulated capital. Successful traders qualify for funded accounts with performance-based payouts. Funding is never guaranteed; it depends entirely on your performance under the published rules.

Which trading platforms does Adiox support?

Adiox connects to MetaTrader 5 (MT5), TradeLocker, and Match-Trader. Challenges run on these platforms, PropSync monitors accounts across them, and the Adiox Copier executes copy-trading and signals to any connected account.

Is copy trading on Adiox safe?

Copy trading always involves risk — past performance never guarantees future results. Adiox reduces avoidable risk: every listed strategy shows a verified track record, your own risk settings always override the provider's, and the Risk Manager can hard-cap exposure per trade. Nothing on Adiox is investment advice.

What is PropSync?

PropSync is a multi-account manager for traders running several prop firm accounts at once. It syncs positions, tracks each firm's rules, warns before daily-loss or drawdown breaches, and pairs with the News Filter to block trades around restricted news events.
