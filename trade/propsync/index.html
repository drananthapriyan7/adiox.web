QUESTIONS

PropSync, answered

How do traders manage multiple prop firm accounts?

Most juggle separate dashboards, spreadsheets, and mental math for each firm's rules — which is exactly how accidental breaches happen. PropSync replaces that with one command center: every account's balance, open risk, daily loss, and distance-to-breach in a single live view, with each firm's specific rules tracked automatically.

Can I see all my funded accounts in one place?

Yes. PropSync connects accounts across MetaTrader 5, TradeLocker, and Match-Trader — regardless of which prop firm issued them — and unifies them into one dashboard with combined equity, per-account health states, and consolidated analytics. Adding an account takes about a minute.

Does PropSync copy trades between my prop accounts?

PropSync includes cross-account trade management, but firm rules come first: many prop firms restrict or prohibit copying between accounts, and PropSync ships with per-firm compliance presets that disable copying where a firm disallows it. You are responsible for following each firm's terms; PropSync makes that easier, never optional.

How do breach-prevention alerts work?

PropSync tracks each account's daily loss and overall drawdown against that firm's specific limits in real time. When any account approaches a threshold you set — say 70% of the daily loss limit — you get an instant alert in the app, by push, or by Telegram, and the account tile turns amber, then red. The warning comes before the breach, not after.
