QUESTIONS

The AI Coach, answered

What does an AI trading coach do?

It reviews every trade you take — entries, exits, sizing, timing — against your own history, finds patterns you can't see (like oversizing after losses), explains them in plain language, and assigns specific drills to fix them. Unlike generic chatbots, it answers from your data: ask why you lose on Fridays and it shows your Fridays.

Can AI analyze my trading mistakes?

Yes, when it has your data. The Adiox AI Coach is wired into your journal, simulator runs, and evaluations, so it can quantify mistakes precisely: which rule you break, under what conditions, and what it costs per month. Its accuracy grows with every trade you log — it is analysis, not fortune-telling.

Is my trading data used to train other people's coaches?

No. Your trades, journal entries, and conversations train your coach only. Aggregated, anonymized statistics (like platform-wide failure rates) may inform published research, but your identifiable trading data is never used to coach other users, never sold, and deletable on request.

Is the AI Coach a signal service?

No. The Coach never tells you what to trade and does not predict markets. It coaches how you trade — risk discipline, pattern awareness, process quality — based on your record. For trade ideas from verified humans, that's the Signal Hub; the Coach's job is making you better at executing your own decisions.
