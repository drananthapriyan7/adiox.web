QUESTIONS

The Academy, answered

Is the Adiox Academy really free?

Yes — all three learning paths, every guide, and the glossary are free forever, with no card required. Adiox earns later in the journey (simulator runs, evaluations, subscriptions), so the education layer has no reason to charge you or hold lessons hostage.

How is the Academy different from YouTube?

Structure and practice. Lessons are sequenced so each builds on the last, and every lesson ends with a hands-on action inside the same platform — learn drawdown, then open PropSim and feel it; learn journaling, then log a real entry. YouTube teaches concepts; the Academy builds habits you immediately use.

How long does it take to become challenge-ready?

The Beginner and Challenge-Ready paths total roughly 15 hours of lessons, but readiness is measured in practice, not watch time: the recommended bar is passing two to three simulated evaluations in PropSim consistently. Most dedicated beginners get there in several weeks to a few months.

Do I get anything for completing a path?

Completion badges and levels appear on your trader profile and next to your name on leaderboards, and the Challenge-Ready certification is one of the qualifying signals for the Pay-Later challenge. The real reward is that your AI Coach uses your progress to calibrate its advice.

Lesson one is waiting. It costs nothing.
