STAGE 01 — BUILD KNOWLEDGE

Learn prop trading from zero — with a coach that never sleeps

What is prop trading education?

Prop trading education teaches traders how proprietary-firm evaluations work — profit targets, drawdown rules, payout structures — alongside core market skills like risk management and trade analysis. Its goal is simple: understand the rules of the game before any money, or evaluation fee, is at stake.

The Learn stage is where every Adiox journey begins, and it stays free forever. Most traders lose money not because markets are impossible, but because they start paying before they start understanding — evaluation fees, signal subscriptions, and losses stack up while the basics are still fuzzy. Adiox inverts that order. The Academy gives you structured learning paths that go from your first candlestick to funded-account rules, the Glossary decodes every term you'll meet along the way, and the AI Coach answers your questions in plain language at any hour. And because Learn is wired into the rest of the ecosystem, what you study connects directly to what you'll practice in the simulator and prove in tournaments — one account, one record of your evolution.

QUESTIONS

Learning on Adiox, answered

Is the Adiox Academy free?

Yes. The Adiox Academy, the trading glossary, and community access are free forever — no card required. You only pay when you move to later stages, such as simulator runs, evaluations, or Pro subscriptions.

Do I need any trading experience to start learning?

No. Academy learning paths start from absolute zero — what a candlestick is, how leverage works, what a prop firm does — and progress step by step toward evaluation-ready skills. Experienced traders can skip ahead using the placement quiz.

What does the AI Coach actually do?

The AI Coach answers trading questions in plain language and, once you start practicing, reviews every trade you log — spotting patterns like oversized risk after winning streaks. It is trained on your own data, so feedback gets sharper the more you use the ecosystem.

How long until I'm ready to attempt an evaluation?

It varies by trader, but the intended path is: finish an Academy learning path, then run simulated evaluations in PropSim until you pass consistently. Most dedicated beginners spend several weeks to a few months in Learn and Train before paying for a real evaluation.
