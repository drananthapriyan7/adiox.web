About Adiox

Trading skill, made competitive.

Adiox is a gamified prop-trading and trading-competition platform. Traders register, fund one wallet and take part in challenges, tournaments and trading games — passing or failing, winning prizes, or becoming funded traders who earn a share of the profit.

What we built

Seven products on one engine

Every product gives you a real trading account on the Adiox Trading Platform, funded with virtual money and watched live. When a rule is broken, the system closes all trades and locks the account automatically — the same way for everyone.

  • Prop-firm challenges, funded accounts and Instant Funded
  • Tournaments, Trading Duels and Trading Battles
  • Single player trading-discipline missions
  • The Training Room simulator, leaderboards and rewards
