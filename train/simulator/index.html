QUESTIONS

PropSim, answered

Can I practice a prop firm challenge before paying for one?

Yes. PropSim recreates prop-firm evaluation conditions — profit targets, daily and overall drawdown limits, minimum days, news windows — on simulated capital for $10 per run. You experience the exact rule pressure of a real challenge, fail safely, and only pay for a real evaluation once you pass consistently.

Why do most traders fail prop firm evaluations?

Mostly on rules, not markets: oversized positions after losses breach the daily drawdown, rushed entries in the first days dig early holes, and news-window violations void otherwise good trades. Simulated evaluation data shows the majority of failures happen on a single bad day — which is precisely what practice runs teach you to survive.

What is a challenge simulator?

A challenge simulator is a training environment that reproduces a prop firm evaluation — same targets, drawdown limits, and time rules — on simulated capital. Unlike a demo account, it enforces the rules and ends the attempt when you breach them, then explains why. It exists so your first real evaluation isn't your first evaluation ever.

Are PropSim rule sets the same as real prop firms'?

PropSim ships with presets modeled on popular industry evaluation formats, including FTMO®-style 2-step rules, plus fully custom rule sets. Adiox is not affiliated with or endorsed by FTMO or any prop firm; presets reproduce publicly documented rule structures so your practice transfers to whichever firm you choose.
