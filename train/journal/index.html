BEHAVIORAL INTELLIGENCE

The patterns have names. Yours are in here.

DETECTED PATTERN

The Revenge Sequence

Loss → re-entry within minutes → size above average → usually a second loss. The engine flags the sequence the moment it starts, not in next month's review.

Fix on file: 30-minute cooldown + size cap after any loss.

DETECTED PATTERN

The Tuesday Tilt

Day-of-week decay is real: some traders' discipline measurably erodes on specific days. Yours might be Tuesday. The heatmap doesn't lie about it.

Fix on file: reduced size or no-trade rule on your flagged day.

DETECTED PATTERN

The Overtrading Creep

Trade count drifting up while average quality drifts down — boredom trades diluting your edge. Detected by comparing frequency against your A-grade baseline.

Fix on file: daily trade budget with a soft-lock warning.

QUESTIONS

The Journal, answered

What should a trading journal track?

At minimum: entry and exit, position size, stop and target, the setup you traded, and the R-multiple result. The insight comes from the second layer — session, hold time, what you did after losses, and whether the trade matched your playbook. Adiox tracks all of it automatically and grades every trade against your defined setups.

Does the Adiox journal auto-import from MT5?

Yes. Connect your MetaTrader 5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader account once and every trade syncs automatically — entries, exits, partials, and swaps included. Simulator runs, backtests, and tournament trades on Adiox file themselves too. No CSV exports, no manual entry, no forgotten trades.

What is revenge trading and how do I detect it?

Revenge trading is re-entering the market quickly after a loss with increased size, trying to win it back. Measurable signs: entries within minutes of a losing close, position size above your average after losses, and abandoning your usual setups. The Adiox behavioral engine flags these sequences automatically and shows you exactly what they cost.

Is the journal free?

The basic journal — manual entries, notes, tags, and core stats — is free forever. Auto-import, full analytics, calendar heatmaps, playbook grading, and behavioral detection are included on the Operator and Professional plans, along with the AI Coach that runs on your journal data.
