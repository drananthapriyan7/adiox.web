QUESTIONS

Strategy Lab, answered

Can I build a trading bot without coding?

Yes. Strategy Lab composes trading systems from visual blocks — indicators, conditions, actions, and risk rules — with no programming at all. The logic you snap together is exactly what runs in the backtester and, when deployed, in the simulator or Signal Hub. If you can describe your rules, you can build them.

What is a rule-based trading strategy?

A rule-based strategy defines every decision in advance: what signals to watch, when a setup is valid, how to enter and exit, and how much to risk. Because the rules are explicit, the strategy can be backtested honestly and executed consistently — removing the in-the-moment improvisation where most discretionary losses come from.

Where can a Strategy Lab strategy run?

Four destinations: run it in PropSim to practice evaluations with it, publish it to the Copy Trading marketplace once its live record qualifies, power a Signal Hub feed for subscribers, or export its signals as alerts to your own platforms. One build, four ways to use it.

Do I have to add risk rules?

Yes — by design. A strategy cannot deploy from the Lab without at least one risk-rule block: per-trade risk cap, daily stop, or max concurrent positions. Backtests will run without them (so you can study raw logic), but nothing reaches real execution paths unguarded.
