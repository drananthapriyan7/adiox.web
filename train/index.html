STAGE 02 — BUILD SKILL SAFELY

Practice the challenge before you pay for it

What is trading practice on Adiox?

Trading practice means rehearsing under real evaluation conditions — profit targets, drawdown limits, daily rules — on simulated capital, so mistakes cost lessons instead of fees. Adiox combines a prop-firm challenge simulator, historical backtesting, and automatic journaling into one connected training loop.

The Train stage exists because of a simple, expensive pattern: most traders' first real evaluation is also their first evaluation ever. They pay full price to discover how drawdown rules feel under pressure — and the market charges tuition without teaching the lesson. Adiox replaces that with deliberate practice. PropSim recreates real challenge conditions for a fraction of the cost, the Backtesting Engine lets you interrogate your strategy against years of history before risking a single simulated dollar, and the Journal captures every trade automatically so nothing is lost to memory or ego. Every rep feeds your AI Coach, which means every failure in here is converted into specific, personal feedback. Fail cheaply, learn precisely, and walk into your real evaluation already knowing how it ends.

QUESTIONS

Training on Adiox, answered

How much does a PropSim run cost?

Simulated evaluation runs start at $10 — a fraction of a typical evaluation fee. Operator and Professional subscribers get simulator runs included monthly. Failing in the simulator costs you a lesson, not a full challenge fee.

How realistic are the simulated evaluations?

PropSim reproduces real evaluation rulesets — profit targets, daily and overall drawdown limits, minimum trading days — including popular industry formats. Prices come from real market data feeds. The one thing it removes is the fee at risk, which is exactly the point.

Does the Trading Journal log trades automatically?

Yes. Trades from PropSim, backtests, tournaments, and connected live platforms (MT5, TradeLocker, Match-Trader) are captured automatically, tagged by setup and session, and scored. Every entry also feeds your AI Coach for pattern analysis.

Can I build strategies without coding?

Yes. Strategy Lab is a no-code builder: define entries, exits, and risk rules with visual blocks, then validate them instantly against the Backtesting Engine. Coded strategies are not required anywhere on Adiox.
