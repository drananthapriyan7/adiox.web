How it works

From purchase to funded

  1. Pick a challenge and size

    Each challenge has packages — for example $10k or $25k — with their own price. Choose a weekly or monthly payout cycle.

  2. Pay once at checkout

    Coupons, bonus credit and wallet cash can all be used. Your account is created on the server you choose.

  3. Trade Phase 1

    Reach the profit target and trade the minimum number of days. Both are needed to pass.

  4. Next phase, free

    Your trades are closed and the next phase account is created automatically with the same package.

  5. Funded request

    After the last evaluation phase a funded-account request is sent to the Adiox team for approval.

  6. Trade funded, get paid

    Keep your login, trade the same loss rules and request a payout each cycle. Payouts →

Rules

The rules of every phase

Each phase sets its own numbers. Break any loss rule and the account is eliminated — trades are closed and the account is locked, with no refund.

Profit target

For example 8%. Equity must reach the starting balance plus that percentage.

Minimum trading days

A day counts as traded when your equity changed compared with the day before.

Overall loss limit

For example 10%. Equity must never fall to the starting balance minus that percentage.

Daily loss limit

For example 5% of the day's opening balance, recalculated each day. Profit made earlier that day gives extra room.

Inactivity

15 idle days in a row eliminates the account. Warnings are sent at 7, 10 and 14 days.

Weekend holding

Shown on each challenge page. Positions may currently be held over the weekend.

How the checks run. Every 2 seconds the platform reads your equity and balance, tracks your worst single-trade loss and lowest equity, and checks the rules in this order: overall loss, daily loss, then profit target. At midnight UTC it records each account's opening balance and counts trading and idle days.

FAQ

Challenge questions

Do I pay again for Phase 2?

No. When you pass a phase your trades are closed and the next phase account is created automatically for free, using the same package.

When do I pass a phase?

When both conditions are met: your equity reaches the profit target and you have traded the minimum number of days. A day counts when your equity changed compared with the previous day.

How is the daily loss measured?

From the day's opening balance, recorded at midnight UTC. With a 5% limit on a $10,000 opening balance, equity must stay above $9,500 that day. Profit made earlier in the same day gives you extra room.

What happens if I break a rule?

The account is eliminated: open trades are closed and the account is locked. There is no refund of the challenge fee.

Can I stop trading for a while?

Up to a point. 15 idle days in a row eliminates the account; you'll receive warnings after 7, 10 and 14 idle days.

What is the payout cycle?

You choose weekly (7 days) or monthly (30 days) when you buy the challenge. Each cycle has its own profit-share percentage, shown on the challenge before you pay.
