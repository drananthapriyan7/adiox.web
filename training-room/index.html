Two ways to train

Preset or custom

Preset simulators

Ready-made rule sets such as Phase 1, Phase 2 and Funded simulators — with a target, overall and daily loss limits, a duration, leverage, an optional consistency rule and the share of the fee you can pay with credit.

Custom simulators

Example

Practise a prop firm challenge in four steps

Here's a custom simulator set up to rehearse a typical Phase 1 with drawdown rules. Then read how to pass a prop firm challenge.

  1. Choose an account size

    For example a $10,000 balance and a leverage from the list.

  2. Set the profit target

    For example 8% — the simulator passes when equity reaches $10,800.

  3. Set the loss limits

    For example a 5% daily loss limit and a 10% overall loss limit — equity floors of $9,500 for the day and $9,000 overall.

  4. Pick the days and start

    30 days costs $10. Trade it on the web terminal and watch each rule's meter live.

Pass & fail

How a simulator ends

With a target

Reaching the profit target passes the simulator — immediately.

Survival mode

No target? You pass by reaching the end without breaking the daily or overall loss limit.

Consistency rule

Optional: no single day may make more than the set share of the total profit. Until that's met, reaching the target doesn't count.

Statuses you'll see: Pending, Active, Passed, Failed, Cancelled and Expired. The settings are copied when your attempt starts, so later changes to a preset don't affect a simulator you're already running.

FAQ

Training Room questions

Where can I practise forex trading with a profit target and a daily loss limit?

In the Adiox Training Room. Choose a preset such as Phase 1, or build a custom simulator with your own balance, profit target, daily and overall loss limits, leverage and number of days. Custom simulators cost $10 for up to 30 days.

Is there a trading simulator with drawdown rules?

Yes — every Training Room simulator enforces a daily and an overall loss limit, and you can add a consistency rule. Breaking a limit fails the simulator, exactly as it would end a real challenge.

Can I win money in the Training Room?

No. Simulators are pass or fail only, with no prizes and no payouts. They exist so you can rehearse a real challenge cheaply.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, if you cancel within 5 minutes of starting. The fee is returned the same way you paid — wallet, credit or both.

Can I use bonus credit?

Each simulator shows how much of its fee can be paid with credit, up to a maximum of 50%.
