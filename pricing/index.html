BILLING QUESTIONS

Pricing, answered

How much does Adiox cost?

Adiox starts free: the Explorer plan (Academy, free tournaments, glossary, basic journal) costs $0 forever, no card required. Operator is $29/month and Professional is $79/month, with two months free on annual billing. Tournaments, $10 simulator runs, and challenges from $89 are priced per event, separately from subscriptions.

Is Adiox free?

Yes — the Explorer tier is genuinely free forever, not a trial: full Academy access, weekly free tournaments with real prizes, the glossary, community, and a basic journal. You only pay when you choose paid events (simulator runs, paid tournaments, challenges) or upgrade for the training and professional tool stacks.

Can I cancel or switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, and your data — journal, records, badges — is retained on the free tier. Annual plans include a 14-day full-refund window.

Do subscriptions include challenges or tournament entries?

No — and that's deliberate, so you never pay for evaluations you're not ready to take. Subscriptions cover tools; challenges, paid tournament entries, and extra simulator runs are purchased per event at published prices. Operator and Professional include monthly PropSim run credits.

14-day refund on annual plans · cancel anytime · prices in USD, taxes may apply by region.
