FAQ

Pricing questions

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. Creating an account is free and there is no platform subscription. You pay per product. The AI Journal, when it opens, will be the only subscription.

Are entry fees refundable?

Not once your account is created and the product has started. Exceptions: Training Room simulators can be cancelled within 5 minutes; game lobbies that expire after 24 hours are refunded; a Duel draw refunds both players minus commission; and a purchase is refunded automatically if the account can't be created.

What currency are prices in?

US dollars.
