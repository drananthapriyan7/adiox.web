Account & Billing

Do I need a card to create an account?

No. The Explorer tier is free forever and requires no payment method. You add a card only when you buy a paid event (simulator run, paid tournament, challenge) or upgrade to Operator or Professional.

Was this helpful?
How do refunds and plan changes work?

Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period, and your data stays intact on the free tier. Annual plans carry a 14-day full-refund window. Event purchases (tournament entries, simulator runs, challenge fees) are refundable until the event or run starts.

Was this helpful?
How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Account → Delete. Deletion removes your journal, coach training data, and personal records permanently within 30 days; anonymized aggregate statistics are unaffected. Export your data first if you want a copy — the export tool is on the same page.

PropSim & Training Tools

How do PropSim run credits work?

Each run is one full simulated evaluation. Runs cost $10 individually; Operator includes 2 per month and Professional includes 4, refreshing on your billing date (unused credits don't roll over). A run ends when you pass, breach a rule, or abandon it — the debrief is generated either way.

Was this helpful?
How do I connect my trading account for auto-import?

Journal → Connections → Add account, then follow the platform flow for MT5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader. Sync is read-only by default and typically completes within a minute; historical trades import up to 12 months back. You can revoke a connection anytime from the same screen.

Was this helpful?
What data does the Backtesting Engine cover?

FX majors and minors, gold and silver, US and EU index CFDs, and major crypto pairs — 10+ years on FX and metals, 8+ on indices, M1 through weekly granularity, with session-accurate spread modeling per instrument.

Tournaments & The Arena

When do free tournaments run?

Daily free tournaments start at 18:00 UTC and run for four hours. Registration is one click and closes at start time. Weekly championships run Friday to Sunday. The full schedule with countdowns is on the Tournaments page.

Was this helpful?
How and when are tournament prizes delivered?

Within 48 hours of results verification. Cash prizes go to your Adiox balance (withdrawable), vouchers and subscription credit apply to your account automatically. Winning accounts are audited before payout; the prize split for every event is published before it starts.

Was this helpful?
How does Arena ranking work?

Five placement matches set your starting division, then an ELO-style rating adjusts after every duel based on your opponent's strength. Divisions run Bronze to Legend and soft-reset each season; your peak emblem stays on your profile permanently.

Challenges, Payouts & Pro Tools

How fast are challenge payouts processed?

Within 48 hours of verification. Request a payout from your funded dashboard; verification re-checks your trades against the rules, then payment is sent. Your first payout on a 2-step account also refunds the original challenge fee.

Was this helpful?
How do I qualify for Pay-Later?

Three passed PropSim evaluations with disciplined drawdown stats (your dashboard shows a live eligibility meter). Qualify, and you can start a standard 2-step evaluation with $0 upfront — 1.5× the normal fee is deducted from your first funded payouts, and you owe nothing if you never reach one.

Was this helpful?
Which firms work with PropSync and Prop Tools?

Any prop firm account issued on MetaTrader 5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader. Rule presets ship for popular firms and are maintained by Adiox; read-only sync is available for firms that restrict third-party order routing. Following each firm's terms remains your responsibility.

Was this helpful?
How do I stop or pause copy trading?

Instantly, from the strategy card or the account's Stop switch: Pause keeps positions open but mirrors nothing new; Stop closes copied positions at market. Nothing is queued while copying is stopped, so when you resume only new trades are copied. Your equity-protection floor does this automatically if losses reach your limit — no master action required.

Adiox Copier — Social, Private & Telegram

What is the Adiox Copier?

The trade-copying engine inside the Trade stage. Social Copier follows ranked master traders, Private Copier copies trades from your own master account to your follower accounts, and Telegram Copier turns signals from the Telegram channels you follow into trades on your MT4 or MT5 account.

Was this helpful?
Which brokers and platforms does the Copier support?

Telegram Copier works with any broker that gives you an MT4 or MT5 account. Social and Private Copier currently support MT5, for both master and follower accounts. You pick your broker server from a searchable list, exactly as it appears in your MetaTrader login window.

Was this helpful?
Do I need to install anything or run a VPS?

Not for Telegram Copier: copying runs on Adiox servers, so there is nothing to install and nothing of your own to keep online. Social and Private Copier plans are either hosted by Adiox or run on your own MT5; each plan states which.

Was this helpful?
How are copied lot sizes calculated?

You choose. Telegram Copier can use a fixed lot, a percentage of balance risked per trade, a lot per fixed amount of balance, or the lot published in the signal, with separate sizing available for gold. Social and Private Copier can use a fixed lot, a multiplier of the master trade, a balance ratio between the two accounts, or a percentage risk.

Was this helpful?
What if my broker uses different symbol names?

Broker suffixes and prefixes such as XAUUSD.m or XAUUSD# are resolved automatically, and common aliases like GOLD, US30 and NAS100 are built in. Indices and unusual instruments can also be mapped by hand.

Was this helpful?
Will copied trades fill at exactly the same price?

Not always. A copied order fills at your broker's price at that moment, so spread, latency and market conditions can move it. A market entry is only valid for about three minutes, so a late signal is skipped rather than filled at a much worse price.

Was this helpful?
What happens if a copy instruction is missed?

The copier regularly compares the positions your account actually holds against what it should hold, and corrects any difference. It catches the rare cases where an instruction does not land, for example if your account was offline when a master closed a trade.

Was this helpful?
Are my broker and Telegram credentials safe?

Broker passwords are encrypted at rest, used only to sign in and start copying, and never written to logs. Masters connect with a read-only investor password. Telegram connects with your phone number and a one-time code; a 2-step password is used once and never stored, and you can end the session any time from Telegram → Settings → Devices.

Was this helpful?
Will a Copier plan renew or charge me automatically?

No. Telegram Copier plans include a 3-day free trial and are paid at checkout by card or crypto. Nothing renews by itself: we remind you before your period ends, and you renew only if you want to.

Was this helpful?
How and when do masters get paid?

A master earns a share of each subscription fee, plus a performance fee charged only on new profit above the copier's high-water mark. Earnings collect in your Adiox wallet and can be withdrawn in USDT (ERC-20 or TRC-20), USDC or through Rise once your identity is verified.
