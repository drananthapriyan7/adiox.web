Questions, answered.

Search below, or browse by topic. Still stuck? Contact support.

Account

How do I create an account?

Register on the portal and confirm your email address — sign-in is blocked until the email is confirmed. Then complete your profile to unlock the $30 welcome credit.

What can I set on my profile?

Your nickname, an avatar (choose a built-in one or upload your own), country and state, language and WhatsApp number. Your country and dialling code are suggested automatically. You can change your password from the profile page too.

Why was I signed out?

Sessions end after 30 minutes without activity. Pages that refresh live, such as account pages, keep your session alive while they're open.

Wallet, deposits & withdrawals

How can I deposit?

By card, by payment link or with crypto. Your wallet is credited when the payment provider confirms the payment, and you'll get an email and a notification.

How do I withdraw?

Complete KYC, then request at least $10 to crypto or a bank account. The amount is reserved from your balance immediately; you can edit or cancel while it's pending, and the Adiox team approves and sends the payout.

What is bonus credit?

Credit you earn from achievements, the welcome bonus, level rewards, points exchange and some prizes. It can pay for part of a purchase — at most 50% — and expires after 30 days. It can't be withdrawn.

In what order is a purchase paid?

Coupon first, then bonus credit up to the cap, then wallet cash, then the payment gateway for anything left (minimum $1). Try the checkout calculator.

What if my account isn't created after I pay?

The purchase is refunded automatically.

Identity verification (KYC)

When do I need KYC?

Only for withdrawals. You can register, deposit and use every product without it.

How does verification work?

Start a verification session from the KYC page in the portal. The result arrives automatically, and you can refresh the status from the same page.

Challenges & funded accounts

How do I pass a challenge phase?

Reach the phase's profit target and trade the minimum number of days, without breaking the overall or daily loss limit. The next phase is then created for free.

How long can I be inactive?

15 idle days in a row eliminates a challenge account. You'll be warned after 7, 10 and 14 idle days.

How do funded payouts work?

When you're in profit and your weekly or monthly cycle has passed, click Request Payout. Trading pauses during review; on approval your wallet receives profit × your payout share and the account resets for the next cycle.

What is the First-Buyer Offer?

A one-time $5,000 challenge account for $4.95 for new buyers, protected by email, IP and device checks.

What is Pay Later?

A challenge you start with a small upfront fee. An activation fee is shown as payable after you pass, and coupons reduce it too.

Tournaments

How are tournament players ranked?

By final equity, highest first, among players who qualify. Players with too few trades, or who missed the target profit when one is set, don't qualify.

Can I rejoin after being eliminated?

If the tournament allows it: buy a new account for the rejoin fee, up to the maximum number of rejoins, after the cooldown period.

How are tied players paid?

Tied players share one rank. Cash or credit prizes for the ranks they cover are pooled and split; a challenge prize goes to each tied player.

Trading games

What's the difference between a Duel and a Battle?

A Duel is exactly two players and can end in a draw. A Battle is three or more players, ranks everyone, and pays only first place.

What if my lobby never fills?

A lobby waiting more than 24 hours expires and every player is refunded. The creator can also cancel before the game starts.

What do single player games pay?

Bonus credit when you pass the mission. If you fail, there's no reward.

Training Room

How much does a simulator cost?

Custom simulators cost $10 for up to 30 days, $15 up to 40, $20 up to 50 and $25 beyond that. Preset simulators show their price in the portal.

Can I cancel a simulator?

Yes, within 5 minutes of starting. The fee is refunded the same way you paid.

Rewards & referrals

How do points and levels work?

Claimed achievements give XP. Exchange 100 points for $10 of credit; your level uses lifetime XP, so exchanging never lowers it.

How much referral commission do I earn?

10% of the cash paid by each of your first 20 referred users and 15% from the 21st on. It's paid to your wallet after a short settling delay and reversed if the purchase is refunded.
