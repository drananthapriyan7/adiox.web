Account & Billing

Do I need a card to create an account?

No. The Explorer tier is free forever and requires no payment method. You add a card only when you buy a paid event (simulator run, paid tournament, challenge) or upgrade to Operator or Professional.

Was this helpful?
How do refunds and plan changes work?

Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period, and your data stays intact on the free tier. Annual plans carry a 14-day full-refund window. Event purchases (tournament entries, simulator runs, challenge fees) are refundable until the event or run starts.

Was this helpful?
How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Account → Delete. Deletion removes your journal, coach training data, and personal records permanently within 30 days; anonymized aggregate statistics are unaffected. Export your data first if you want a copy — the export tool is on the same page.

PropSim & Training Tools

How do PropSim run credits work?

Each run is one full simulated evaluation. Runs cost $10 individually; Operator includes 2 per month and Professional includes 4, refreshing on your billing date (unused credits don't roll over). A run ends when you pass, breach a rule, or abandon it — the debrief is generated either way.

Was this helpful?
How do I connect my trading account for auto-import?

Journal → Connections → Add account, then follow the platform flow for MT5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader. Sync is read-only by default and typically completes within a minute; historical trades import up to 12 months back. You can revoke a connection anytime from the same screen.

Was this helpful?
What data does the Backtesting Engine cover?

FX majors and minors, gold and silver, US and EU index CFDs, and major crypto pairs — 10+ years on FX and metals, 8+ on indices, M1 through weekly granularity, with session-accurate spread modeling per instrument.

Tournaments & The Arena

When do free tournaments run?

Daily free tournaments start at 18:00 UTC and run for four hours. Registration is one click and closes at start time. Weekly championships run Friday to Sunday. The full schedule with countdowns is on the Tournaments page.

Was this helpful?
How and when are tournament prizes delivered?

Within 48 hours of results verification. Cash prizes go to your Adiox balance (withdrawable), vouchers and subscription credit apply to your account automatically. Winning accounts are audited before payout; the prize split for every event is published before it starts.

Was this helpful?
How does Arena ranking work?

Five placement matches set your starting division, then an ELO-style rating adjusts after every duel based on your opponent's strength. Divisions run Bronze to Legend and soft-reset each season; your peak emblem stays on your profile permanently.

Challenges, Payouts & Pro Tools

How fast are challenge payouts processed?

Within 48 hours of verification. Request a payout from your funded dashboard; verification re-checks your trades against the rules, then payment is sent. Your first payout on a 2-step account also refunds the original challenge fee.

Was this helpful?
How do I qualify for Pay-Later?

Three passed PropSim evaluations with disciplined drawdown stats (your dashboard shows a live eligibility meter). Qualify, and you can start a standard 2-step evaluation with $0 upfront — 1.5× the normal fee is deducted from your first funded payouts, and you owe nothing if you never reach one.

Was this helpful?
Which firms work with PropSync and Prop Tools?

Any prop firm account issued on MetaTrader 5, TradeLocker, or Match-Trader. Rule presets ship for popular firms and are maintained by Adiox; read-only sync is available for firms that restrict third-party order routing. Following each firm's terms remains your responsibility.

Was this helpful?
How do I stop or pause copy trading?

Instantly, from the strategy card: Pause keeps positions open but mirrors nothing new; Stop closes copied positions at market. Your equity-protection floor does this automatically if losses reach your limit — no provider action required.
