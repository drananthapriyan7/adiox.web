Lifecycle

From announcement to prizes

Every tournament moves through the same stages. The status on its card tells you where it is.

  1. 1

    Coming soon

    Published with its start and end time, rules and prize table. You can join already.

  2. 2

    Joined · pending

    Accounts made before the start wait as pending and switch on when it begins.

  3. 3

    Live

    Everyone trades the same starting balance. Rules checked every second, ranks every minute.

  4. 4

    Registration closes

    Joining stops at the cutoff time shown on the tournament — before the end.

  5. 5

    Ended

    Non-qualifying players are removed and final ranks are set by equity.

  6. 6

    Prizes & close

    Prizes paid by rank. Tournament accounts are disabled 3 days after the end.

Rejoin

Eliminated while the tournament is live? Buy back in for the rejoin fee, where it's enabled.

How rejoining works

  • Only while live — rejoins aren't possible before the start or after the end.
  • Cooldown — wait the cooldown period shown on the tournament after elimination.
  • Limit — each tournament sets a maximum number of rejoins per user.
  • Fresh start — the account is reset to the starting balance and new credentials are emailed.

How joining works

  • Before the cutoff — and while slots are available.
  • Pay once — the entry fee is taken at checkout ($0 for free tournaments).
  • Get your account — login and password are shown in the portal and emailed to you.
  • Trade on the web terminal — press Start Trading to open the Adiox Trading Platform.

    Ranking & prizes

    How ranks and prizes are decided

    Cash

    Paid to your Adiox wallet. Spend it on any product or withdraw it after identity verification.

    Bonus credit

    Covers part of a future purchase (up to 50%). Credit expires after 30 days.

    Challenge account

    A free prop-firm challenge account of the size set in the prize table.

    Ranking

    • Qualifying players are ranked by final equity, highest first.
    • While live, ranks refresh every minute.
    • Equal equity means an equal rank, and the next rank is skipped — 1, 1, 3.

    Ties and prizes

    • Cash and credit: the prizes for every rank the tied players cover are pooled and split equally.
    • Challenge accounts: every tied player receives one — nothing is split.
    • Prizes are paid automatically or by the Adiox team, depending on the tournament.

    FAQ

    Tournament questions

    Are Adiox tournaments free?
    Free tournaments have a $0 entry fee; paid tournaments show their fee before you join. Both rank players the same way and pay prizes by rank.
    Is the money in a tournament account real?
    No — tournament accounts are funded with virtual money on the Adiox Trading Platform. Prizes are real: cash and bonus credit go to your Adiox wallet, and some ranks win a free challenge account.
    How are winners ranked?
    By final equity, highest first, among players who qualify. Players with fewer than the minimum trades, below the target profit (when one is set), or with $0 equity or less don't qualify.
    What happens if two players finish with the same equity?
    They share the rank and the next rank is skipped. Cash and credit prizes for the ranks they cover are pooled and split equally; a challenge-account prize goes to each of them.
    Can I join after a tournament has started?
    Yes, until the registration cutoff shown on the tournament and while slots are available.
    Can I rejoin after being eliminated?
    Where the tournament allows it: while it's live, after the cooldown, up to the maximum number of rejoins, for the rejoin fee. The account is reset to the starting balance.
    Are news trading, copy trading and HFT allowed?
    Each tournament page states whether news trading, copy trading and high-frequency trading are allowed for that event. Check before you trade.
    What happens to my account after the tournament?
    Final ranks are set and prizes are paid. Tournament accounts are disabled 3 days after the end.
