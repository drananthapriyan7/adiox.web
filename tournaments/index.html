How it works

A tournament, start to finish

  1. Join

    Free tournaments cost $0; paid ones charge an entry fee at checkout. Each tournament shows its slots and join cutoff time.

  2. Wait for the start

    Accounts made before the start wait as pending and switch on automatically when the tournament begins.

  3. Trade to the end

    Everyone has the same starting balance. Loss rules are checked every second while you trade.

  4. Qualify

    At the end, players with too few trades — or who missed the profit target, when one is set — are removed from the ranking.

  5. Ranked by equity

    Qualifying players are ranked by final equity, highest first. Ranks update every minute while it runs.

  6. Prizes paid

    Prizes are paid by rank, automatically or by the Adiox team. Accounts are disabled 3 days after the end.

Rejoins

Knocked out? Buy back in.

Where a tournament allows it, an eliminated player can buy a new account for the rejoin fee — up to a set number of times, after a cooldown period.

  • Rejoin fee, maximum rejoins and cooldown are shown on each tournament.
  • Some tournaments let you hold more than one account at the same time.
  • Tournaments are the only product with rejoins and multiple accounts per user.

Prizes

Prize tables across many ranks

Each rank range gets its own prize, in one of three forms.

Cash

Paid to your wallet balance — spend it on any product or withdraw it.

Bonus credit

Credit that covers part of a future purchase. Credit expires after 30 days.

A challenge account

A free prop-firm challenge account — start Phase 1 without paying the entry fee.

Tie rule. Tied players share one rank. A cash or credit prize is pooled across the ranks they cover and split between them — two players tied for 1st split the 1st and 2nd prizes. A challenge prize is given to each tied player.

FAQ

Tournament questions

Where can I join a free forex tournament, and how are winners ranked?

On Adiox: open the tournaments list in the portal and join any tournament with a $0 entry fee before its cutoff time. Everyone starts with the same balance; at the end, players who made the minimum number of trades (and hit the target, when one is set) are ranked by final equity, highest first, and prizes are paid by rank.

Is this a demo trading contest?

Tournament accounts are funded with virtual money, so you never risk your own trading capital. Prizes are real: cash and bonus credit go to your Adiox wallet, and some ranks win a free challenge account.

Can I join after the tournament starts?

Until the join cutoff time and while slots are available. If you join before the start, your account waits as pending and switches on when the tournament begins.

How is the daily loss measured in a tournament?

From yesterday's closing balance, in dollars. Tournament rules are set as dollar amounts rather than percentages.

Why was I removed from the final ranking?

At the end, players who made fewer than the minimum number of trades, or who missed the target profit when one is set, do not qualify for a rank.

When are prizes paid?

After the tournament ends and ranks are final. Depending on the tournament, prizes are distributed automatically or by the Adiox team.
